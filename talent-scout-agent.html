How it works
STEP 01
JD parsing
Extracts role title, required skills, experience range, must-have criteria, and company context from free-form text.
STEP 02
Candidate discovery
Searches a structured candidate database first. Supplements with AI-simulated profiles if fewer than 5 matches are found.
STEP 03
Match scoring
Scores each candidate 0–100 on skill alignment, experience fit, must-have coverage, and growth potential.
STEP 04
Interest simulation
Simulates a personalised outreach exchange with each candidate and scores their likely interest level 0–100.
Match score weight
60%
